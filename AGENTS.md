# Project working instructions

## Evaluation and correction

Before reviewing, generating, replacing, or admitting film footage, read
`docs/EVALUATION-LOOP.md` and follow its intent-led evaluation loop.
Use `docs/templates/SHOT-REVIEW.md` for each correction and its subsequent takes.

Understand the useful intent before prescribing a fix. State inferred intent as
a hypothesis, not knowledge of a model's internal reasoning. Diagnose observed
failure mechanisms, preserve what worked, and give the generator a coherent
physical and narrative solution rather than only prohibitions.
Communicate the apparent intent as outside feedback, not just an internal guess:
agreement can validate that the purpose is legible; disagreement can reveal that
the method communicates something different. Use that discrepancy constructively
without insisting on the inferred intent or dismissing the observed effect.

Review the preceding shot, entire candidate, following shot, and corresponding
story passage. A good performance or clean motion diagnostic cannot override
broken blocking, object mechanics, anatomy, continuity, or meaning.

Carry each failure and acceptance condition into the next iteration; do not
restart evaluation from the latest candidate's most attractive moment.
Do not change sentence-driven runtime logic to repair an authored film.

Keep review playback muted and do not interrupt the user's viewing tabs.
