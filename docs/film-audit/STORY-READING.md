# Independent reading: The Gift of the Magi

7 September 2026. Read the complete twelve units of the app's story, then the
complete story in O. Henry's *The Four Million*, Project Gutenberg ebook 2776,
https://www.gutenberg.org/files/2776/2776-h/2776-h.htm (The Gift of the Magi section).
This interpretation precedes the new frame audit. It does not admit existing takes.

## Dramatic argument

The gifts are materially unusable precisely because each spouse privately gave
up the possession the other wanted to honour. Their apparent foolishness reveals
their generosity. Neither sacrifices to extract gratitude. The recognition must
feel affectionate, not like punishment, humiliation, a prank, or a product reveal.
The ending offers ordinary shared life, not a magical restoration of possessions.

## Complete reading, in order

- s1: Repeated counting establishes months of effort and insufficient money.
  Della's disappointment is about giving Jim something worthy, not wanting luxury.
- s2: The narrator looks away toward their modest household and diminished income.
  The habitual welcoming hug establishes love already present; it is not today's
  surprise arrival. Any illustrative memory must be legible as such.
- s3: Crying ends; powder rag, window, grey backyard and cat precede the pier glass.
  Loving thought becomes a decision. She moves to the glass BEFORE releasing her
  long hair. Shining eyes and sudden pallor hold determination and cost together.
- s4: The two treasures are her knee-length brown hair and his inherited gold
  watch. Sheba and Solomon are narrator comparisons, not actual visitors. Her
  nervous re-pinning and momentary tears prevent the sacrifice feeling effortless.
- s5: Brown jacket and brown hat; apartment exit DOWN to street; then UP one flight
  to Sofronie's shop. Hat removal releases long hair. Businesslike appraisal,
  twenty-dollar offer, urgent acceptance. The cutting is implied, not described
  on the page: a permissible bridge must not invent a new emotional event.
- s6: Search compresses two hours. One restrained platinum fob chain is chosen
  because its quiet worth resembles Jim. Purchase costs $21, leaving 87 cents.
  The imagined watch-and-chain future is conditional, not an actual fitting.
- s7: Back home, Della curls the short hair STILL ATTACHED TO HER HEAD. Forty
  minutes pass. Critical mirror inspection reveals vulnerability beneath resolve.
  The schoolboy/chorus-girl comparisons do not introduce other characters.
- s8: At seven, coffee is ready and the hot pan awaits chops. She doubles the
  chain, sits at the table corner by the door, hears the stairs and prays. Jim
  enters and closes the door; his stare is unreadable, explicitly not anger.
  He is 22, in a worn overcoat, WITHOUT GLOVES. Family does not imply a baby.
- s9: Della approaches him, explains, seeks reassurance. Jim cannot reconcile the
  loss with his gift. Her 'boy' is a loving address to her adult husband. Serious
  sweetness culminates in his embrace. The narrator discreetly looks elsewhere:
  the inconsequential object must not become a ghost or a symbolic spectacle.
- s10: Parcel comes FROM HIS OVERCOAT POCKET, is tossed ON THE TABLE, then her
  hands undo string and paper. Recognition: delight -> grief -> his comfort.
  Side and back tortoiseshell combs have jewelled rims suited to her lost hair.
  Her hug of the combs, tearful smile and hair-regrowth reassurance precede her
  sudden recollection that she has not yet given him his present.
- s11: The same chain rests in her open palm; she eagerly offers a future she
  believes possible. She asks for the watch. Jim instead settles on the couch,
  hands behind head, smiles, and discloses its sale. They keep both gifts; the
  watch is not recovered. His suggestion to cook the chops restores domestic ease.
- s12: The narrator names the paradox: their sacrificial generosity is wisdom.
  'Children' describes the couple; the biblical Babe is not their child. Preserve
  intimate recognition through the conclusion, then the agreed graceful black.

## World state versus knowledge

Jim's exact sale time offscreen is unspecified, but by his arrival the watch has
been sold to fund the combs. Della does not learn this until s11. Do not use the
disclosure timestamp as the time the watch physically disappears. Likewise her
long hair remains gone after the sale even when the narrator remembers it.

## Persistent entity contract

Story fixes meaning/material; the inventory audit will choose the visual design.
Do NOT declare the most recent generation canonical merely because it is recent.

| ID | Invariants | Legitimate changes / forbidden resets |
|---|---|---|
| DELLA | Same adult woman, brown hair, devoted and resourceful | Long pinned -> released -> pinned -> sold short -> curled short. No regrowth during present action. |
| JIM | Same young adult husband, worn overcoat, bare hands | Enters, stands, embraces, finally sits. No extra spouse, child or late gloves. |
| WATCH | One inherited gold pocket watch, physically attached bow/crown | Present in exposition; absent by Jim's arrival through ending. No watch attached to final chain. |
| CHAIN | One simple platinum fob chain, restrained design | Folded/doubled/open-palm are poses, not length, link, clasp or ornament changes. |
| COMBS | One side-and-back set, tortoiseshell, jewelled rims | Packaging opens and custody changes; silhouette, teeth, count and jewel pattern persist. Story does not specify exact count: choose it from compatible inventory and lock it. |
| PARCEL | One plausible coat-pocket package for the combs | Pocket -> table -> opened wrapping -> revealed contents. No resetting unopened/held state. |
| HAT-JACKET | Old brown hat and brown jacket | Worn outdoors; hat removed at shop. Same construction and colour across journey. |
| FLAT | Same modest room, pier glass between windows, door/table/stove/couch geography | Camera may move but doors, knobs, windows and object placement may not teleport. |
| SUPPER | Coffee ready, hot pan awaiting chops | No cooked feast before the concluding invitation to cook. |

Visual IDs will include reference frame, crop, source hash, distinguishing
features and confidence. Unknown/occluded is not absent, and appearance mismatch
under a new angle is not automatically morphing.

## Adaptation versus transcription

The app's s2 drops the original clause about the letters of Dillingham looking
blurred, replacing that image with a shortened sentence. There are spelling and
punctuation differences too. Record these; do not synthesize a voice repair or
claim verbatim fidelity without a separate text/audio alignment pass.

## Emotional direction and spending rule

Preserve expressive urgency, attentive listening, mutual reassurance, ordinary
touch and humour. A costly beautiful insert is expendable if it contradicts the
gift. A technically clean shot can still be irrelevant or emotionally vacant.
Finish the whole-film review and inventory comparison before any reshoot. Rank
story contradictions and continuity failures ahead of decorative improvements.
