# The Gift of the Magi — award candidate

Updated 6 September 2026. Working production authority, not a claim of selection,
eligibility, independent endorsement, or an award.

## Aim and current position

Make a moving, coherent short film that can compete on filmmaking, not merely
demonstrate an AI pipeline. The dramatic spine is Della choosing to risk how she
is seen in order to express love, followed by the recognition of Jim's matching
sacrifice. Protect that performance and the warmth of their relationship.

Current public baseline: **v0.9.6-portfolio**, approximately 14:51, native 24 fps,
1920×1080, stereo. Keep it stable while the candidate is edited and reviewed.
The technical delivery is not proof of artistic readiness. The previous review
sampled the whole film and examined specific defects densely; it was not a full
uninterrupted, sound-on independent screening.

## Work order and release gates

1. **Departure and hair sale, 4:46–5:37.** Replace the inward departure and
   disappearance/reappearance. Eliminate the shop-stair reversal and the hat
   coming off twice/returning for the haircut. Cut on the decision to accept the
   price; an ellipsis can carry the haircut into the next scene. Preserve urgent
   acting without impossible geography. Review this entire passage against the
   preceding hair-pinning shot and following shopping scene.
2. **Waiting, approximately 7:00–8:00.** Shape a progression from repair, to
   self-conscious inspection, to fear of Jim's reaction. Repeated gestures need
   different dramatic purposes or replacement coverage, not automatic equal
   shot lengths. Keep the curling tool on attached hair and preserve short-hair
   continuity. Do not shorten the narration or accelerate footage to hide gaps.
3. **Reunion and gifts.** Preserve the now two-person embrace and ordinary
   privacy cutaway; recheck them in the final master. Let recognition, confusion,
   and relief motivate cuts. Recheck watch hardware, hat, chain, combs, hands,
   contact and all adjacent shots at full detail. Equal shot durations are a
   review signal, not an automatic defect or a reason to cut arbitrarily.
4. **Sound pass.** Replace chapter-local score restarts with an authored musical
   arc. Retain the approved chamber palette and clear narration. Spot subtle
   room tone, cloth, coins, footfalls, door, paper and clock where the picture
   supports them. No added voices or modern jingles. Save dialogue, score and
   effects stems. An actual sound-on listening pass on headphones and speakers
   is mandatory; loudness measurements cannot approve a dramatic mix.
5. **Picture and delivery lock.** Native motion, ordinary cuts, no reversals,
   loops, frozen padding or speed fitting. Intentionally composed dissolves may
   soften a transition but cannot disguise broken blocking. Check the complete
   exported film and captions, including title-to-counting transition and final
   fade to black. No app timing logic may drive the movie.
6. **Independent screening.** With the user's approval, obtain at least one
   experienced editorial review and feedback from viewers who have not followed
   production. Ask where attention drops, what physical action was unclear, and
   whether the sacrifice and final recognition move them. Record timestamps and
   disagreements, not a manufactured numerical claim that the film will win.
7. **Submission package and approval.** Verify eligible categories, public-online
   policy, current deadlines, fees, prior selections, actual screening and jury.
   Prepare synopsis, accurate director biography, credits, stills, production
   journal, AI disclosure and documented rights/provenance. Confirm filmmaker
   residence and country of production with the user. Submit only after approval
   of each entry, its terms and fee. Do not unpublish the portfolio film to meet
   a premiere rule without approval.

## Approval and evidence boundaries

The award outcome depends on an external jury. No award, acceptance, submission,
fee payment, rights clearance or independent review has happened under this plan.
Generation is a candidate, not admission. A repaired passage is not a verified
whole-film master. Local renders, publication and festival acceptance are separate
states and must be reported separately.

Follow [the intent-led evaluation loop](EVALUATION-LOOP.md) and retain rejected-take
reasons. Never trade continuity for a beautiful frame or lower acceptance standards
because a generation has already cost credits.
