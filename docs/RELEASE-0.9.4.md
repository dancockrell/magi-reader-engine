# 0.9.4 — a music-led prologue

## Intent and correction

It seemed the previous edit intended to get the audience quickly into Della's
story. The result compressed the title into a label and did not provide the
separate opening movement the director requested. Preserve the book and score,
but give the title its own composition, time and musical space.

The new prologue is fifteen seconds without narration or story captions:

- Near-black field; warm ivory Georgia title in two levels, with muted gold author.
- Title elements fade in separately with a restrained twelve-pixel upward drift.
- At five seconds the book begins a one-second reveal beneath the departing title.
- The complete native ten-second book take plays once, without the previous jump cut.
- Picture dips through black over the book's last 0.75 seconds and the story's
  first 0.75 seconds. No frozen gap, reversed frames or footage speed changes.
- First voice and caption at 15 seconds, over the pennies; Della at 17 seconds.
- Existing instrumental score leads the prologue, then lowers smoothly into narration.

The ending now fades picture and lettering together over two seconds, with a
short black tail and a three-second musical fade. Runtime: 896.906 seconds,
1920x1080, 24 fps. Relative to 0.9.3, all 244 captions move +11.25 seconds;
story wording, later chapter timing and the watch correction remain intact.
