# 0.9.6 — animated opening typography

The previous small, single-line overlay was readable but did not make the title
the main event. This revision changes both hierarchy and motion, not story timing.

- Two-line title at 200/180 design pixels rather than one line at 86 pixels.
- Staggered entrances: each line rises about 90 pixels and grows from 84% to 100%,
  resolving from a soft blur into sharp type over 1.7 seconds.
- Gold rule expands from its center; the author follows in warm gold.
- A feathered upper lighting field subdues the bright windows without a banner.
- On exit the title grows slightly, softens and fades; the lighting field clears
  with it. Both are gone before the illustration reveal.

The book moves from the first frame, music remains continuous, and the direct
0.75-second illustration-to-pennies dissolve still starts at 9.25 seconds.
Narration, captions, full duration and final two-second fade to black are unchanged.
No loops, holds, reversed footage, speed fitting or extra generation credits.

Review: full-size composition and ordered quarter-second animation states; title
rule alignment corrected after first render. This is a composited motion-graphics
treatment of the existing book take, not newly generated footage. Other pending
film-shot corrections are not represented as completed by this release.
