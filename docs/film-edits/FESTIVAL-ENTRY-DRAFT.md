## Logline

With Christmas approaching and almost nothing to spend, a young wife sacrifices
her greatest treasure for her husband's gift, unaware that his love has led him
to a sacrifice of his own.

## Short synopsis

In a modest New York apartment, Della counts the coins she has saved for Jim's
Christmas present. They are not enough. Determined to give him something worthy
of her love, she sells her long hair to buy a chain for his treasured watch.
Back home, she tries to repair her appearance and waits, afraid of how he will
see her. Jim's arrival brings an unreadable silence, then the revelation of two
gifts made unusable by the sacrifices that bought them. Adapted from O. Henry's
story, this narrated period film finds its drama in small gestures: counting,
choosing, waiting, and finally recognizing a love neither gift can contain.
